Alerts

Choose how you want to be told when a share stops working, is fixed, or the panel updates. Use one or more.

📱 Phone notification (easiest, free)

  1. On your phone, install the free app ntfy (App Store or Google Play).
  2. Make up a private topic name that nobody could guess, e.g. sam-nas-7f3k9q (anyone who knows it can read the alerts), and type it below.
  3. In the ntfy app tap +, type the same topic name, tap Subscribe.
  4. Click Save and send test at the bottom of this page - your phone should beep.

✉️ Email

Gmail: server smtp.gmail.com, port 587, username = your Gmail address, password = an app password (Google Account → Security → App passwords), not your normal password.
